01 · The market

AEC is not like other markets

Five structural facts that define how marketing must work here. If we ignore these, nothing else makes sense.

High-value strategic projects

Each project represents years of revenue. Quality of positioning matters more than volume. Being everywhere weakens the message.

Value over volume

Long cycles. Decisions made upstream.

18 to 36 months from first signal to contract. The decision is made well before the RFP. Sometimes before the project even has a name.

Decision before the RFP

QBS. Credibility before price.

Qualifications-Based Selection. You do not win on price. You win on reputation, references, and trust built over time.

Reputation before price

Many decision-makers per project

5 to 15 people involved in each decision. Engineers, directors, procurement, elected officials. Each has different priorities.

Map the decision team

A relationship market. Digital amplifies.

Trust takes years to build. Digital does not replace the relationship. It prepares it, amplifies it, and documents it. An event without digital follow-up has a 48-hour shelf life.

Digital amplifies the relationship
